About

Research at the intersection of public finance and public administration

I am a PhD candidate in Public Affairs at Indiana University Bloomington, specializing in public finance, with additional training in policy analysis and data science. My research lies at the intersection of taxation, state capacity, and public sector governance, particularly in developing economies.

My current work examines how tax administration reforms, digital enforcement technologies, and institutional changes shape tax compliance and government revenue mobilization. I am also interested in tax policy, public debt, public budgeting, and the effectiveness of public institutions.

I bring approximately a decade of professional experience in Pakistan's Inland Revenue Service, working on tax policy implementation, administration, and enforcement across diverse economic sectors. This experience informs my research and motivates my interest in rigorous, policy-relevant evidence on strengthening fiscal institutions.
